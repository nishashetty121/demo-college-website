Curriculum & Admissions

Discover our academic roadmap and learn how to enroll your child into Pinecrest's legacy.

Grade-Level Tracks

🧡

Elementary School (Grades K-5)

Focuses on building core foundational literacy, mathematical reasoning, scientific curiosity, and collaborative social skills. We emphasize play-based learning alongside structured conceptual milestones.

Early Literacy Creative Arts Basic Coding Foreign Languages
⚖

Middle School (Grades 6-8)

Bridge the gap between foundation and deep application. Students transition to subject-specific educators, engaged project layouts, analytical sciences, pre-algebra structures, and active sports leagues.

Robotics Lab Critical Debate Pre-Algebra Environmental Science
💻

High School (Grades 9-12)

College preparatory environment featuring Advanced Placement (AP) curriculums, dual enrollment options, creative studio arts, vocational electives, and structured university guidance programs.

AP Courses University Prep Advanced STEM Leadership Seminars

Admissions & Enrollment Guide

Follow our streamlined 4-step admissions process designed to help us understand your child's needs and aspirations.

1

Online Inquiry

Submit a general application inquiry via our Contact Form. Our representative will share the digital admission package and answer initial questions.

2

Application Submission

Complete the student admission forms, providing official transcripts, teacher recommendation letters, and previous assessment results.

3

Evaluation & Interview

Students participate in a grade-appropriate diagnostic assessment and an informal online or in-person interview with our faculty team.

4

Enrollment Offer

Successful candidates receive an official admissions offer. Complete registration by submitting fee structures and signing policy agreements.
