About Pinecrest Academy

A heritage of academic excellence, personal growth, and integrity built over decades of dedication.

Founded on the principles of holistic education, Pinecrest Academy has been a beacon of academic achievements and community leadership. We nurture critical thinkers, passionate learners, and compassionate human beings who are ready to make a significant impact on global scales.

Our expansive campus, cutting-edge facilities, and exceptional educators provide an optimal atmosphere where students discover their unique talents and shape their future endeavors.

Principal's Welcome

"Welcome to Pinecrest Academy. Here, we believe that education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge, but the ignition of a lifelong passion for discovery. Our goal is to foster an environment where every student feels valued, challenged, and inspired to reach their full potential. I invite you to explore our campus and join our vibrant family."

Our History & Milestones

Revisit the historic achievements and moments that defined Pinecrest Academy since inception.

1998
01

Foundation of Pinecrest Academy

Pinecrest was established with just 80 elementary students and a single academic building, driven by a vision of holistic developmental education.

2005
02

Campus Expansion & Middle School Addition

Acquired 15 acres of surrounding land to establish custom science laboratories, a state-of-the-art library, and the Middle School division.

2012
03

High School Division & First Graduating Class

Inaugurated our High School building and celebrated our first graduating class, who went on to enter top national universities.

2020
04

STEM & Creative Arts Center Inauguration

Opened the multi-million dollar STEM & Arts Complex, housing advanced computer labs, robotics workspaces, and a 500-seat theater.
